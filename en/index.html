How it works

From voice note to text in 3 steps

No setup, no bots, no copy-paste. Matt lives in the iPhone Share menu.

  1. Long-press the voice note

    Long-press the voice message and tap Forward (WhatsApp) or Select (Telegram). You can pick multiple voice notes at once.

  2. Share with Matt

    Tap the Share icon and choose Matt. Transcription starts instantly: language and speakers are detected automatically.

  3. Read, summarize or translate

    In seconds you get the full text. One tap to summarize, translate, copy or save it.
