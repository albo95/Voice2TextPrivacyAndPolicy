How it works

From voice note to text in 3 steps

No setup, no bots, no copy-paste. Matt lives in the iPhone Share menu.

  1. Long-press the voice note

    In WhatsApp or Telegram, long-press the voice message you want to read and tap Forward → Share.

  2. Choose Matt

    Pick Matt from the iOS share sheet. Transcription starts instantly, right on your iPhone.

  3. Read, summarize or translate

    In about 3 seconds you get the full text. One tap to summarize, translate, copy or save it.
