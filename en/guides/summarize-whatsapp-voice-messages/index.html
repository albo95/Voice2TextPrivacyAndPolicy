How to summarize WhatsApp voice messages with AI

Updated July 2026 · 3 min read

There's the long voice note, and then there's the long voice note: 7 minutes of monologue where the useful information amounts to three things. Transcribing helps, but reading a wall of text isn't much better than listening. The fix is the AI summary: the key points of the message in a few lines.

Summarize a voice note in 4 steps

  1. Share the voice note with Matt (long-press → Forward → Share → Matt);
  2. Matt transcribes it in about 3 seconds, on your iPhone;
  3. Tap Summary;
  4. Choose the detail level: Essential, Concise or Detailed.

The 3 detail levels

  • Essential — the gist in 2-3 points: perfect for instantly knowing whether it needs a reply right now;
  • Concise — the complete key points: the ideal balance for work voice notes;
  • Detailed — everything that was said, organized as bullet points: great for meetings and instructions.

Not just voice notes

The same AI summary works on endless text messages (share them with Matt from the chat), on infinite threads and on imported files: meeting recordings, lectures, voice memos, .txt and .pdf documents. See also how to transcribe audio files on iPhone.

🔒 Privacy note: transcription stays 100% on-device. Only when you request a summary is the text (never the audio) processed in the cloud, anonymously. Details in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I also summarize long text messages?

Yes: share any text (or import a .txt/.pdf file) with Matt and get a key-point summary.

Does the summary work offline?

The AI summary uses the cloud for top quality; on iOS 26+ Matt can use on-device Apple Intelligence as an offline alternative. Transcription, by contrast, always works without a connection.
