How to transcribe WhatsApp voice messages on iPhone

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

A 7-minute voice note while you're at work. Three audios in a row in the family group chat. A voice message you can't play because you have no headphones. If you use WhatsApp, there's one real fix: transcribe voice messages to text and read them in seconds.

This guide shows you how to do it on iPhone with Matt, an app that turns any voice note into complete text in about 3 seconds, without leaving WhatsApp and without sending your audio to any server.

Transcribe a WhatsApp voice note in 5 steps

  1. Download Matt for free from the App Store and open it once to activate it.
  2. In WhatsApp, long-press the voice message you want to read and tap Forward (the arrow).
  3. Select the voice note — you can tick several at once — and tap the Share icon at the bottom.
  4. In the iOS share sheet, choose Matt (the first time, scroll the apps or tap "More").
  5. Done: the full transcription appears in about 3 seconds, ready to read, copy, summarize or translate.
💡 Tip: the first time you open the share sheet, Matt may not be in the front row. Scroll the apps, tap "More" and drag Matt to the top — next time it'll be one thumb-tap away.

Why not just use WhatsApp's built-in transcription?

WhatsApp includes a voice note transcription feature, but it has well-known limits: it leaves dashes and skipped words when the audio isn't perfect, supports fewer languages, and stops at plain transcription. Matt uses Apple's best speech recognition and adds what's missing:

  • No-gap transcriptions, even with fast or noisy audio;
  • AI summaries with key points for really long voice notes (how it works);
  • Real-time translation from 40+ languages (translation guide);
  • A searchable archive of every saved transcription.

Privacy: where does the audio go?

By default, nowhere: transcription runs 100% on-device — entirely on your iPhone — and works offline too. In this mode no audio is uploaded to external servers, and the sender gets no notification. Only if you request an AI summary is the text (never the audio) processed in the cloud; and only if you enable Advanced Transcription (Premium) is the selected audio sent to our provider AssemblyAI, with your consent.
