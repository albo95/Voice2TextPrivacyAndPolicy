Convert WhatsApp voice messages to text: every method compared

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

There are several ways to convert a WhatsApp audio to text: WhatsApp's built-in feature, bots you forward messages to, web services, and dedicated apps. They are not equal — neither in text quality nor in privacy. Here's the honest comparison.

1. WhatsApp's built-in transcription

WhatsApp lets you transcribe a voice note by long-pressing it and choosing "Transcribe". It's convenient, but in practice it has three limits:

  • when the audio is fast or noisy it leaves dashes and skipped words;
  • supported languages are limited;
  • you only get raw text: no summary, no translation, no archive.

2. Bots and web services

Forwarding a voice note to a bot or uploading it to a website means sending the audio (and the sender's voice) to third-party servers. Beyond the privacy issue, the flow is slow: forward, wait, go back to the chat. Not recommended for personal or work messages.

3. A dedicated app with native sharing (Matt)

Matt converts voice notes to text using Apple's speech recognition, entirely on your iPhone. The flow is as short as it gets:

  1. Long-press the voice note in WhatsApp;
  2. Tap Forward → Share → Matt;
  3. Read the full text in about 3 seconds.

And once converted, the text is actually useful: you can copy it, save it to a searchable archive, summarize it with AI or translate it.

Quick comparison

  • Text quality: Matt (no gaps) > native WhatsApp > bots;
  • Privacy: Matt (100% on-device, offline) > native WhatsApp > bots/web services;
  • Extra features: only Matt offers AI summaries, 40+ language translation and an archive.
