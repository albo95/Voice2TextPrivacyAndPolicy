How to read WhatsApp voice messages without listening to them

Updated July 2026 · 3 min read

You're in a meeting and a 4-minute voice note arrives. You're in class, in the library, on public transport with no headphones. Or you simply don't want everyone around you hearing your business. In all these cases the question is the same: how do I know what a voice note says without playing it?

The answer: you read it. With Matt you turn the voice note into text in 3 seconds, in total silence, without leaving WhatsApp.

Read a voice note silently, step by step

  1. Install Matt (free) on your iPhone;
  2. Long-press the voice note in WhatsApp → Forward → Share;
  3. Choose Matt in the share sheet;
  4. Read the full text of the message: the audio is never played.

Why it beats turning the volume down

  • Zero audio: the voice note is never played, not even at volume zero. Nobody around you hears a thing.
  • Zero notifications to the sender: transcription happens on your phone; whoever wrote you gets no alert.
  • Faster than listening: read a 3-minute voice note in 20 seconds. And if it's endless, summarize it with AI into key points.
  • Actually private: everything runs on-device, even offline. The audio never leaves your iPhone.
💡 Typical scenario: work group chat, 6-minute voice note from your boss. Share → Matt → "Essential" summary: 5 key points in 10 seconds, and nobody in the office notices.
